# SmartFund Personal Finance – User Research Survey

## INTRO

**Hello, and thank you for your time!**

We're building a Personal Finance feature inside **SmartFund**, a financial management platform for Nigerians. This survey helps us understand your banking habits, what frustrates you about managing money, and what features would genuinely improve your financial life.

This survey takes approximately **4–5 minutes** to complete. Your responses are **completely anonymous**. We will not share your individual answers with any third party. Any contact info you provide at the end is only used to invite you to a follow-up interview — you can skip it.

By continuing, you consent to your anonymized responses being used for product research purposes.
